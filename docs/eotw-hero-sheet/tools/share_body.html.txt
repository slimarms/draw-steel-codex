    <section class="note">
      <h2>What is different for EotW, and why</h2>
      <ul>
        <li><b>Read-only by design.</b> The sheet only has the actions the rules allow: equip or unequip treasure, spend a Recovery outside combat by clicking the Recoveries ring, change appearance, and edit in the builder before the Hero wins an encounter. Nothing edits Stamina or other numbers, so EotW's "enforce everything" holds.</li>
        <li><b>The sheet shows the Hero; the town changes them.</b> Levelling up, taking a respite and the Hero's history belong to places in town (a Training Grounds and so on). The sheet points there: "Head to the Training Grounds to Level your Hero."</li>
        <li><b>Built for your levelling answer.</b> Victories sit beside Recoveries, because pushing on with Victories banked and Recoveries low is now the player's decision. The XP bar shows, hatched, the XP the next respite will add, and says when a respite would Level the Hero. At Level 10 the bar becomes the class's epic resource.</li>
        <li><b>One sheet everywhere.</b> The Guild, the town strip and the party view open this sheet, and teammates' Heroes open read-only, in town and in an encounter. The other ways into the old editable sheet (the C key, the token menu, search, chat commands) would point here.</li>
        <li><b>The character panel stays the combat companion.</b> Conditions and effects live there; the sheet's numbers match the panel's. If something waits on you while the sheet is open, its top bar turns gold and says what (a trigger, a save, a roll, the story continuing, or the Heroes being able to act).</li>
        <li><b>It looks like EotW.</b> The hero card, enlarged, anchors the sheet with the same stamina bar, recoveries ring, resource and surge icons as the HUD card. Panels are frosted glass over where you are: the Hero's Guild in town, the battle map in an encounter.</li>
        <li><b>Everything on one screen.</b> No tabs: abilities and features side by side, and the left column never scrolls. Hovering shows the same ability card the action bar uses; clicking pins it.</li>
        <li><b>Laid out the way you use it.</b> Characteristics sit with skills, grouped by skill group, because a test is a characteristic plus a skill. Abilities follow the feature tags, including each Hero's heroic abilities and triggered actions, plus the standard actions and free strikes every Hero has.</li>
        <li><b>Treasure you can read.</b> Leveled treasure (with the carry-three count), trinkets and consumables. Hovering an item shows the value it would change in gold; equipping changes it. Armor or weapons the kit cannot use say "No benefit", and leveled treasure shows its Level 5 and 9 text.</li>
        <li><b>Every state handled.</b> Away in a party, dead mid-encounter, fallen (opened from the Graveyard as a memorial), Danger Rooms practice, loading (a skeleton of the whole layout) and a failure to load. Reduce Motion, Font Size up to 140% and the Status Icons setting are all respected.</li>
      </ul>
    </section>
    <section class="note">
      <h2>Questions for you (rules calls; none changes the layout)</h2>
      <ul>
        <li><b>What carries home after an encounter.</b> Pushing on only costs something if spent Recoveries and Stamina come home with the Hero. Today nothing spent comes home. Should it? And consumables used or won?</li>
        <li><b>Carrying three leveled treasures.</b> Hard cap, allow it with a warning, or automate the respite Presence test?</li>
        <li><b>Equipping while a party forms.</b> The City refuses changes to a Hero claimed by any party, so a player waiting at the Gate cannot equip the treasure they just won. Heroes are copied in at Begin, so allowing equip and appearance changes until then would carry in safely. Lock only once the game is underway?</li>
        <li><b>Edit in Builder.</b> Your doc says before a Hero's first encounter; the sheet allows it until the Hero wins one, so a Hero who lost stays rebuildable. Which do you want?</li>
        <li><b>Town places.</b> The sheet points to a Training Grounds for levelling, a respite, and a Record location for the Hero's history. Where do these live, and what does a respite offer once respite activities exist (and how often)?</li>
        <li><b>Encounters for levelled Heroes.</b> Every Hero is still clamped to Level 1 on arrival; levelled Heroes need level bands or scaling.</li>
        <li><b>Your hero card file.</b> Three small changes would carry to the sheet: the Status Icons shapes on the stamina bar, the damage wash at most once a second at 30% (flashing safety), and how temporary Stamina is drawn (it disappears at full Stamina today).</li>
        <li><b>Closing the other routes.</b> Pointing the C key, token menu, search and chat commands at this sheet touches core code. Flagging it before the build gets there.</li>
        <li><b>Still noticed.</b> The AI's "Waiting for..." banner hides whenever any panel window is open, and the EotW character panel's view-only mode blocks play actions such as the Summoner's Sacrifice Minions.</li>
      </ul>
    </section>
