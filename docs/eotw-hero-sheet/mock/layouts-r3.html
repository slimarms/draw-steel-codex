EotW sheet layouts

Round 3 wireframes: three ways to arrange the hero sheet, drawn at 1920x1080 with the same pregen data as the round 2 mock. All three follow the rules agreed so far. The sheet looks the same in town, montages and encounters. Skills sit beside the characteristics. Anything on the hero card looks the same here. The abilities and features lists keep their shape.

Why columns: the August top zone was a row of blocks holding different amounts of content. A row is as tall as its tallest block, so short blocks sat half empty while narrow ones got squeezed. A column lets each block take its own height. Each concept stacks the blocks differently.

1. Stat spine

Everything is on screen at once. Every number lives in one column read top to bottom: stamina, then tests, then combat stats, then gear. The left column is who the hero is.

Strengths
  • Skills are always in the same place, right under the characteristics.
  • Nothing is behind a click.
  • The spine has room to grow at higher levels (more immunities, more treasure).
Failure modes
  • Four columns can read as busy.
  • The two lists get narrower, so long ability rows wrap sooner.
  • On a narrower screen the spine would have to fold away.

2. Big card

The left column is the EotW hero card, enlarged: characteristics down the art's edge, skills under the name, stamina, recoveries, resource and surges on the bottom plate. Players see the same card on the HUD and the montage stage. Movement, potency, defences and gear sit in a narrow details column.

Strengths
  • Learned once, read everywhere: card and sheet match by construction.
  • The art is the frame, not a separate box.
  • Characteristics and skills sit together on the card, where montages already use them.
Failure modes
  • Text over art depends on the plate's contrast.
  • The card holds a fixed amount, so a long skill list crowds it.
  • Combat stats are split away from stamina.

3. Rail and pages

A rail holds what you check constantly: portrait, stamina, tests and combat stats. The rest of the screen shows one page at a time: Abilities, Features, Gear or Story. Pages get the full width, so abilities show as full statblocks with no clicking.

Strengths
  • No cramping: each page answers one question.
  • Gear and the equip call to action get a whole page, and the tab shows a count.
  • Room for EotW-only content (victories record, story) without squeezing anything.
Failure modes
  • Abilities and features are no longer side by side.
  • One click to switch pages.
  • At level 1 the Abilities page is sparse, and the rail is already full (it scrolls).

Stamina bar study

S1 is the hero card's bar as built; the sheet reuses it. S2a to S2c are candidates for a better shared bar, so each is shown at sheet size and at card size. On the current bar, temporary stamina only gets the room left over after current stamina, so a full hero's temp shows only as text.

Stand-ins: Bitter replaces Berlingske Slab; the diamond replaces the heroic resource icon; "Lightning Treads" is a sample treasure for the equip prompt. Free strikes and the standard abilities are not in the data export, so Abilities lists each hero's own abilities only. All on-screen wording is placeholder copy for sign-off later.
