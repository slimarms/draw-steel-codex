  • Accepted decisions: "A respite would Level this Hero." and a 10px XP bar with a notch (Progress > A respite would Level them); hovering an item shows the value it would change, already changed and in gold, and equipping changes it for real, holds it gold for a moment, then it settles (Veteran inventory, then hover or Equip Lightning Treads: Speed); surges tooltip with numbers; near-opaque panels and a dark top-bar band over battle maps; feature text about 75 characters a line, state words upright, Standard actions collapsed to start.

Skin proposal: motion and sound

  • Open (Motion > Open the sheet): the backdrop fades and the sheet rises 10px, 200ms. Sound UI.WindowOpen; Close plays UI.WindowClose.
  • Switch hero (click a thumbnail): the card art fades in, 180ms; every value lands at once. Mouse.Click.
  • Equip (Veteran inventory, then Equip on Lightning Treads): the row and every value the item changes glow gold for a second (Speed 6 to 8). UI.Inv_Place; Unequip plays UI.Inv_Grab.
  • Damage and healing in a fight: the hero card's own red or green wash and the bar slides, 450ms. No sound: the fight already plays its own.
  • Ready to level: one light sweep along the gold bar the first time you see it. No sound: the celebration belongs to the Training Grounds, where the level-up happens.
  • Values arrive after loading: they fade in, 160ms. A blocked control while away plays UI.Error_Generic beside its reason.
  • Every other press plays Mouse.Click; nothing plays on hover. Reduce Motion (the Codex setting) turns every animation off and the shimmer and pulse go still. The toast in the corner names each sound.

Skin proposal: palette (fixed dark, no themes)

  • Text and chrome: the builder's cream (#F3EDE7 headings, #DFCFC0 body), tan #BC9B7B for stat labels, muted #9a9188, on near-black frosted panels over the scene.
  • Gold #ffd66b (the hero card's gold) means one thing: act on this or something landed - Equip, ready to level, surges, the "you're needed" Close, the change glow. Never decoration.
  • Blue #6fa8ff = yours (thumbnail ring, owner chip). Stamina green / amber / red come from the hero card's bar. The town's antique gold #d9b56a is not used on the sheet, so there is one gold.

Abilities follow the tagging plan

  • A feature tagged Ability or Trigger shows as an ability card here; its feature row stays hidden.
  • Prose features that the codex implements as an ability stay visible features. The Conduit's Life and War Domain Effects are now in Features (Combat), not Abilities.
  • Kit signature abilities, core-feature actions such as Mark, free strikes and standard actions have no tagged feature, so they always show.
  • Triggered actions come from a separate list in the codex; round 5's data had missed them.

Stand-ins

  • Bitter replaces Berlingske Slab. The hover card imitates the codex ability card; the real one is CreateAbilityTooltip.
  • The Human Censor's two test abilities ("Halt Miscreant! - Copy", "Test custom trigger") are left off: they are custom test content on that pregen.
  • The veteran inventory, many immunities, progress and second melee kit are simulated with real item and kit records.
  • All on-screen wording is placeholder copy for sign-off later.
