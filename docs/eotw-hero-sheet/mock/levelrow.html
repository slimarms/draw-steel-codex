The level row on the hero card

Version 2, after David's answer: heroes level as in Draw Steel. Victories become XP at a respite, and taking a respite is the player's choice, so a hero can set out again with high Victories and low Recoveries. Victories therefore now drive a decision (extra heroic resource at the start of every combat versus fewer Recoveries) and have to be visible. Each column is one option and each row a moment in a hero's life. Hover any bar or number for its tooltip; the tooltip text is also written out under each card. Every gold or cream sentence is a [COPY] draft for sign-off, and "Training Grounds" is a placeholder name.

Surges, now that Victories sit beside Recoveries

The row now holds Recoveries, Victories and the heroic resource. Surges only exist in combat, and on the real hero card they are not in a row at all: they float in the card's corner, one icon per surge, none at 0, up to 9. With 9 surges the row has room for about two of them, so they need a place of their own. Corner keeps the hero card's rule; Count trades the icons for one icon and a number; Own line adds a line to the plate during a fight.

What differs between the options

  • Victories beside Recoveries puts the two numbers a player weighs before setting out side by side: what they have banked against what they have left. The level row keeps only progress, with the hatched stretch showing how far the next respite will take them.
  • Victories on the level row is round 10 plus the hatched stretch. Victories sit with XP, the thing they turn into; the trade-off with Recoveries is two rows apart.
  • Round 10 as it was: the number only. Nothing shows whether a respite would level the hero.

The same in every option

  • Ready to level turns the bar and the next level gold and adds one line pointing to the town place that does it. The sheet never levels a hero.
  • When a respite would be enough to level, the hatched stretch reaches the end of the bar and the next level brightens.
  • Level 10 drops the bar and says it is the highest level.
  • 16 XP per level (Heroic Advancement). Numbers are illustrative.
