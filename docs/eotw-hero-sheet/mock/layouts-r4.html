What changed from round 3

  • Characteristics use full names and moved off the card into the tests block, next to skills grouped by skill group.
  • Movement, potency, immunities and weaknesses sit at the head of Abilities, beside the actions that use them.
  • Gear sits under level progress in the left column, with the equip prompt where you see it.
  • Level progress shows XP toward the next level, with Victories beside it.
  • Job colours are gone. Every block uses the same padding and fills its width.

Things to check

  • Does the tests block read as one block, or as another busy top zone?
  • Switch to the Orc Conduit (level 6) for a fuller ability and feature list.
  • Switch to the Polder Shadow for a teammate's read-only sheet.
  • Try the stamina states with each bar style, then compare them side by side in the study below.

Stand-ins

  • Bitter replaces Berlingske Slab; the diamond replaces the heroic resource icon.
  • Free strikes and standard actions are the Dwarf Fury's from the live app, shown for every hero.
  • "Lightning Treads" is a sample treasure; "Some progress" sets 11 XP and 2 Victories.
  • All on-screen wording is placeholder copy for sign-off later.

Stamina bar study

Temporary stamina is lost first, so each T variant draws temp as the part of the bar damage reaches first. Every variant shows the winded mark and the dead value (a hero dies at the negative of their winded value). Each is shown at sheet size and at hero card size.
