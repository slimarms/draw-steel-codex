What changed from round 4

  • Size, speed, disengage, stability, potency, immunities and weaknesses moved into the stats band; three arrangements to compare.
  • Skills and languages are inline lists, still grouped by skill group. The characteristics explainer is gone.
  • Ability rows show the action type and the cost together. Hover for the card, click to pin.
  • The card plate spells out recoveries (10/10, +11 each) instead of the ring.
  • Feature sections collapse, the filter sits with the tags, and long features expand.
  • Gear: "Kit" is the heading; kit-less heroes show their prayer, ward, enchantment or augmentation; the Tactician shows both kits.

Decisions made for you (check these)

  • The Conduit's domain effects sit in their own group, "Domain effects", noting when they are used, not under triggered actions.
  • Abilities stay grouped by action type; the jump chips at the top scroll to each group.
  • Where both Tactician kits give the same bonus, the lower one is struck through. Only the damage bonus is a choice, switchable in town.
  • Treasure splits into worn, carried and consumables. Armor that does not match the kit is flagged.

Stand-ins

  • Bitter replaces Berlingske Slab. The hover card imitates the codex ability card; the real one is CreateAbilityTooltip.
  • Free strikes and standard actions are the Dwarf Fury's from the live app, shown for every hero.
  • The veteran inventory, many immunities, progress and second melee kit are simulated with real item and kit records.
  • All on-screen wording is placeholder copy for sign-off later.

Stamina bar study (for the David conversation)

The sheet uses S1, the hero card's bar as built, for now. The T variants are kept for the conversation with David: temporary stamina is lost first, so each draws temp as the part of the bar damage reaches first. Every variant shows the winded mark and the dead value.
